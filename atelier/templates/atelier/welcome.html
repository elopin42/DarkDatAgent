What happens on your computer

Everything runs on your computer. The video file goes to Gladia, which transcribes the voice. The keyframes and sentences go to H Company, which writes the procedure and runs the agent. The first video takes a few minutes: download, transcription, keyframes, storyboard. You can follow each step as it runs.

Why

Companies already have years of training videos and vendor tutorials. This turns them into agents that follow your rules, and tells you when a video is no longer enough. Read about the project.
